# Job Scout never contacts anyone or applies

Job Scout only suggests: leads to consider, and people the job seeker could reach out to, with a reason for each. It never sends DMs, emails or connection requests, and never submits applications. The job seeker does all outreach and applying by hand. This is a deliberate boundary, not a missing feature. Automated outreach from a job seeker's own accounts risks those accounts and reads as spam to the people contacted. The same rule is why X is read with a Bearer token that cannot send DMs (see ADR 0004).
